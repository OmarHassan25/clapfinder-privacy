Clapfinder Privacy Policy

App: Clapfinder: Find My Phone (com.goapps.clapfinder) · Developer: Omar Hassan
Contact: goapps500@gmail.com · Last updated: October 8, 2026

In short: Clapfinder listens for claps and whistles on your phone only while you turn listening on. Audio is analysed in real time on your device and immediately discarded. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob.

Microphone

When you turn on listening, the app uses your phone's microphone to detect claps and whistles. Audio is analysed in real time on your device, held in memory for a fraction of a second, and then discarded. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent to us or anyone else. Listening runs only after you start it, a notification is shown the whole time, and you can stop it at any time.

Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, or receive any personal data. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and certain US states, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Purchases

The optional "Pro" upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app only receives confirmation that you own Pro, which is stored on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone: clap and whistle detection (see above).
  • Notifications: listening status, the "I found it" control, and the "tap to resume" prompt after a restart.
  • Run at startup: only to show the "tap to resume" prompt after a restart; the microphone is not started automatically.
  • Vibration: alert vibration.
  • Internet: used only by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

Data deletion

All app data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. For data processed by Google for ads, use Google's ad settings or the links above. Questions: goapps500@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed.
